Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about contact, support, and travel planning.

How soon will I get a response?

We typically reply within one business day. For time-sensitive travel questions, call our team during office hours.

Can I modify or cancel my booking?

Yes. Send your booking reference and the change you need. Available options, fees, and refunds depend on your airline and fare rules.

Do you offer travel planning for groups?

Yes. Tell us your destination, preferred travel dates, group size, and budget so our team can help you plan.

Can I get help with a visa application?

Our team can explain the application process and the documents generally required. Visa decisions remain with the relevant embassy or immigration authority.

What information should I include in my message?

Include your travel dates, destination, number of travelers, and booking reference if you have one. Please do not send payment card details or passport scans through this form.

Do you provide support outside office hours?

You can email us at any time. Our regular office hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM–7:00 PM. Messages outside these hours are reviewed on the next business day.
